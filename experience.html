Our work and experience

KZ was founded in 2024. This page shows the work KZ has done since then, followed by the experience our team brought with them from earlier roles.

KZ projects

Since 2024, KZ has partnered with schools in Panama, Egypt, Venezuela, Turkey, and the United Kingdom, and our programmes have reached more than 1,000 educators. Our workshops cover curriculum and instruction, assessment and feedback, and systems thinking, and we coach school leaders one-on-one.

Student learning communities programme

A multi-phase programme for Grades 3 to 6, with a workshop series, teacher tools, and an implementation plan. Keri Zeller designed the programme, and the KZ team delivered it at a partner school.

Assessment and feedback workshop

A hands-on workshop on feedback routines and assessment design, designed and delivered by Keri Zeller. It is built around application, so teachers leave with strategies they can use in their own classrooms.

Relationships and behaviour at an international school in Egypt

The school wanted stronger relationships between students and teachers, better communication between students, and fewer behaviour problems. KZ advised on the school's personal, social, and emotional learning curriculum, with a focus on emotional intelligence, communication, and life skills. We supported staff in building stronger connections with students, guided teachers in using behaviour data to reflect on classroom dynamics, and strengthened the systems for recording student concerns so teams could follow up clearly.

Grade-level teams reported fewer behaviour problems and a clearer sense of how to respond to student needs as they arise.

Detailed write-ups and samples are on Keri Zeller's portfolio
