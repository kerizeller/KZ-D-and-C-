Formats

Single workshop

One focused session of 1.5 to 3 hours, or a half day, on a single priority.

Professional learning day

A whole-group opening session, then smaller sessions for different teams or divisions. Often delivered by two facilitators.

Programme with coaching

A series of sessions over a term or a year, with coaching and observation between sessions so new practice takes hold.

Train-the-trainer

We design the sessions and prepare your own people to deliver them, with facilitator guides and practice.

How we plan a workshop

  1. Before

    A planning call about your goals and participants. We adapt examples and tasks, and send any materials in advance.

  2. During

    At least half the time is practice on participants' own work. Everyone leaves with a tool or product they will use.

  3. After

    A follow-up conversation about what changed, and suggestions for next steps.

Sample day for a school

  • Morning: whole-staff session to build a shared focus
  • Late morning: separate sessions for primary and secondary teachers
  • Afternoon: session for leaders and coordinators on supporting the change
  • Afterwards: follow-up call and planning for next steps

Sample half day for a team

  • Opening: goals for the session and real situations from the team
  • Practice: short input, then practice on those situations with feedback
  • Planning: each person writes what they will try, with whom, and by when
  • A few weeks later: check-in with the manager on what changed

Questions

Can sessions be adapted?

Yes. Every session is adapted to your context, and sessions can be combined into a half day, a full day, or a series.

Can you deliver online?

Yes. We deliver in person in the UAE and online.

What do participants take away?

Each session ends with a tool or product, such as a planning template, a revised lesson or plan, or a personal action plan.

How do we get started?

Contact us with your goals, the group, and your preferred dates. We will suggest sessions and a format.

Talk to us
