How we work with you

  1. A first conversation

    We talk about your goals, your people, and what you have tried so far.

  2. Find out what is needed

    Interviews, surveys, or listening sessions, scaled to the size of the work.

  3. Design and deliver

    Sessions built around real tasks, delivered in person, online, or by your own trainers.

  4. Follow up

    We check in with managers and participants to see what changed, and adjust.

Questions

Do you work with schools outside the UAE?

Yes. KZ has partnered with schools in Panama, Egypt, Venezuela, Turkey, and the United Kingdom. We work in person and online.

Can you run a whole professional learning day?

Yes. A common format is a whole-staff opening session followed by smaller sessions for primary, secondary, and leadership teams.

Do teachers work on their own materials?

Yes. Teachers bring their own units, lessons, or student work, so they leave with something they can use the next day.

Planning professional learning?

Tell us about your school, your priorities, and your dates. We will suggest a session or programme that fits.
