How we work with you

  1. A first conversation

    We talk about your goals, your people, and what you have tried so far.

  2. Find out what is needed

    Interviews, surveys, or listening sessions, scaled to the size of the work.

  3. Design and deliver

    Sessions built around real tasks, delivered in person, online, or by your own trainers.

  4. Follow up

    We check in with managers and participants to see what changed, and adjust.

Questions

Do you work outside Abu Dhabi?

Yes. We work in person in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and across the UAE, and online with organisations in other countries.

Can you deliver training in your own sessions and also train our people to deliver?

Yes. We can facilitate sessions ourselves, or design the programme and prepare your own trainers to deliver it.
