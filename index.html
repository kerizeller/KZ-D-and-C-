Recent project

Student learning communities, Grades 3 to 6

A partner school wanted to build student-centred learning communities in Grades 3 to 6, where students have more voice, agency, and ownership of their learning. Some elements were already in place, but there was no shared model.

KZ ran a two-day workshop series for leaders and teachers, then stayed on site to coach early practice in classrooms. Leaders and teachers agreed on the key systems and routines, identified priority areas for further support, and left with a phased implementation plan for the following year.
