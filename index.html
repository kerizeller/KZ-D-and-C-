Training, onboarding, and AI upskilling for organisations, schools, and universities in the UAE.

KZ Development & Consulting is a learning and development consultancy in Abu Dhabi. We begin each project by finding out what people need to do differently in their jobs, and we build the training around that.

Contact usView services

Since 2024, KZ programmes have reached more than 1,000 educators at partner schools in Panama, Egypt, Venezuela, Turkey, and the United Kingdom.

How we help

We help with problems like these. New staff take too long to settle in. Managers were promoted for their expertise and now need to lead. Teams are starting to use AI without shared guidance. Or the organisation has grown and training is still informal.

We work on each of these through training needs analysis, onboarding programmes, leadership and communication training, AI upskilling, and setting up an L&D function. Schools and universities also work with us on curriculum and assessment.

Our founder

Keri Zeller has spent 18+ years designing learning in the United States, Albania, Mexico, Uzbekistan, and the UAE. Before founding KZ, she set up the faculty development and student success departments at Webster University Tashkent, supporting more than 175 faculty and 40 staff. She holds an M.Ed. from Vanderbilt University, has completed her Ed.D. dissertation at Johns Hopkins University (conferral December 2026), and holds a Certificate in Management & Leadership from Harvard Business School Online.
