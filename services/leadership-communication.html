Services

Leadership and communication

Practical development for managers and teams who need to lead, present, and give feedback well.

Managers are often promoted for their expertise and then expected to lead without support. We help them build the everyday skills that make teams work: clear communication, useful feedback, and confident presentation.

Programmes combine short workshops with practice on real situations and one-on-one coaching between sessions.

What this can include

  • Manager development programmes
  • Presentation and public speaking skills
  • Giving and receiving feedback
  • Systems thinking for leaders
  • One-on-one coaching

We work with organisations in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and across the UAE, in person or online.

Our experience

KZ coaches school leaders one-on-one and runs workshops on feedback and systems thinking. Keri Zeller also teaches public speaking and presentation skills at Abu Dhabi Polytechnic, and taught leadership and public speaking at NYU Abu Dhabi Summer Academy.

Questions

Who is this training for?

New and experienced managers, team leads, and staff who present or give feedback as part of their role.

Is coaching included?

Programmes can include one-on-one coaching between sessions, so managers can work on real situations.

Can the training be delivered online?

Yes. We deliver in person in the UAE, online, or as a mix of both.
