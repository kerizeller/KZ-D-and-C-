Services

Team effectiveness

Support for teams under pressure or going through change.

When teams struggle, the cause is often communication and trust rather than skill. We work with leaders and teams to improve how people communicate, resolve disagreements, and adapt to change.

What this can include

  • Conflict resolution and mediation
  • Emotional intelligence and communication workshops
  • Change support: aligning teams and keeping people informed

We work with organisations in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and across the UAE, in person or online.

Our experience

Victor Simone leads this work. He has over 16 years of experience in mediation and team development, an M.Ed. in International School Counseling, and certifications in mediation, executive negotiation, and emotional intelligence.

Questions

Who leads this work?

Victor Simone, a conflict resolution specialist with over 16 years of experience and certifications in mediation, executive negotiation, and emotional intelligence.

Is this for teams in conflict or for all teams?

Both. We support teams going through a disagreement or a change, and teams that want to communicate better.

Can sessions be delivered online?

Yes. We work in person in the UAE and online.
