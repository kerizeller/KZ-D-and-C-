Education is where KZ began, and it remains a large part of our work. We support school and university leaders with curriculum, assessment, and the professional learning that helps teachers put changes into practice.

Our team includes specialists in IB programmes and in ADEK compliance and inspection readiness.

What this can include

  • Curriculum review and design
  • Assessment and feedback systems
  • IB authorisation and ADEK inspection readiness
  • Workshops, multi-phase programmes, and leader coaching
  • Faculty development in higher education

We work with organisations in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and across the UAE, in person or online.

Our experience

KZ has partnered with schools in Panama, Egypt, Venezuela, Turkey, and the United Kingdom. Our team includes a former IB coordinator and a specialist with more than 16 years of curriculum and ADEK compliance work in Canada and the UAE.
