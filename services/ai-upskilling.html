AI upskilling

AI training and guidelines shaped by what your teams already use and worry about.

On this page

  • What this can include
  • Our experience
  • Workshops and tools

Most AI training starts with a tool demonstration. Ours starts by listening, because people arrive with very different experience, expectations, and concerns.

We run listening sessions, then design training around the real tasks of each team, and help leaders put clear guidelines in place for responsible use.

What this can include

  • Listening sessions on staff AI use and concerns
  • Team-specific AI training built around real tasks
  • Responsible-use guidelines and policy
  • Follow-up support for managers

We work with organisations in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and across the UAE, in person or online.

Our experience

Our team has run staff listening sessions on AI, designed department-specific AI training from the findings, and helped write a K–12 AI policy. We also work with university faculty on AI integration.

Questions

Why start with listening sessions?

Staff arrive with very different experience of AI and different concerns. Listening first means the training answers the questions your people have.

Can you help us write an AI policy?

Yes. We help leaders put responsible-use guidelines and policy in place alongside the training.

Do you work with schools as well as companies?

Yes. We work with organisations, schools, and universities.
