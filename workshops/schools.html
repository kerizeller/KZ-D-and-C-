Workshops

Workshops for schools

Practical professional development for teachers, coordinators, and school leaders. Teachers work on their own lessons and units, and leave with tools they can use the next day.

32 sessions. Select a title to see details.

How sessions work

Each session can run on its own or as part of a professional learning day or year-long programme. We adapt every session to your curriculum, priorities, and staff, and we can deliver in person in the UAE or online. Many schools combine a whole-staff session with smaller sessions for primary, secondary, and leadership teams.

Teaching and learning

Differentiation in action2 to 3 hours

Practical, low-preparation ways to plan for different readiness levels, learning profiles, and interests. Teachers work on their own lesson or unit plans and redesign part of an upcoming lesson.

Best for: Primary and secondary teachers

Participants leave with: A differentiation planning template and a revised lesson segment

Using data without overwhelm1.5 to 2 hours

Simple formative checks and reflection routines that make classroom data useful for daily planning. Teachers design exit tickets and a weekly data-to-action cycle.

Best for: All teachers, especially upper primary and secondary

Participants leave with: A data tracker and a quick weekly reflection guide

Designing for access (UDL)2 to 3 hours

Universal Design for Learning and co-planning with EAL and learning support teams, so support is built into lessons from the start rather than added at the last minute.

Best for: Classroom teachers working with EAL, learning support, and inclusion leads

Participants leave with: A universal access planning tool and a modified lesson segment

Assessment with purpose2 to 3 hours
